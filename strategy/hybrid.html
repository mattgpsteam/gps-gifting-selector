The Hybrid Gifting Model

Sometimes the answer is both

This brief is for the property that doesn't fit cleanly into one camp — strong guest participation and the budget to justify a high-perceived-value in-hand gift, but also a database broad enough that choice matters and a calendar with room for more than one gifting moment. 

Traditional continuity delivers better value at lower price points; digital gifting delivers a seamless experience with less overhead. Savvy operators leverage both to build a complete loyalty program.

How It Works

Coordinated, Not Layered

The two formats aren't redundant — they do different jobs and run on different days.
One drives the visit, the other monetizes it.

+

A high perceived-value, in-hand gift that pulls guests onto the property on giveaway day.

Every Other Day · Digital
Monetizes it

An earn-and-get store runs every day that isn't a continuity day — rewarding play and giving guests a reason to qualify again.

Both are sourced and themed together each month — one creative cycle covering two formats, not two programs to manage.
The Proof Is in the Pairing

A Northern California casino began pairing traditional and digital gifting after a 10-year gap. It raised the budget to one $35 weekly continuity gift and added a weekly earn-and-get reward tiered at $30 and $75. Two formats, one creative cycle — and the combination averaged $300,000 in revenue per month.

Another Way to Structure It

Treat Your VIP Guests Like VIPs

Segment your top loyalty tiers out of continuity and simply give them a better gift — not the same one the middle tier picks up each week. No play-up, no extra qualification. It's recognition: we see you, and here's something worthy of your tier.
