The Traditional Gifting Model

Why traditional gifting is still right for your property

Digital gifting is having a moment. Dropship and online-redemption programs are everywhere — and they carry real advantages. But the pitch skips a hard number: what your gifting budget is actually worth by the time it reaches the guest. If your property runs $10–$25 weekly gifts and already hands them out in person on event day, traditional continuity stretches every dollar further than dropship. Here's why:

The Math

Tried, Tested, Strategy Approved

In a dropship program, soft costs stack on top of every gift. Traditional continuity is bought in bulk — better product pricing, no door-to-door shipping, no per-gift tax, because guests redeem in person. On a $20 budget in Michigan, the difference is stark.
