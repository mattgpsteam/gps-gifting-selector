The Digital Gifting Model

Digital gifting is a genuine step up — for the right property

Digital gifting is already here — an online dropship continuity that's quick to build, easy to run, and lets each guest choose from a range of items, taking the storage-and-staffing burden off your event days. It's the right fit if you're constrained on space or labor, serve a wide database where one gift never fits everyone, or want to run gifting without a warehouse operation behind it.

How It Works

A Digital Revival

Each month the store is curated fresh — travel essentials one month, bedding and décor or kitchen gadgets the next. The guest goes home with a gift they'll actually use, and that satisfaction reflects back on your brand.

Insight to Know
Start reinvestment low, build it over time.

Digital's biggest strength — variety — is also its catch. Guests see items side by side each month and develop a sharp sense of value fast, even with no price tags anywhere. Start reinvestment conservatively and build it up; pulling back once guests are used to a level costs more in redemptions and sentiment than starting low ever would.
