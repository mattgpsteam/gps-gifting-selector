The Digital Gifting Model

Digital gifting is a genuine ROI driver — for the right property

Digital gifting is a different loyalty strategy, not a digital version of continuity. It's the engine behind an earn-and-get, VIP reward, play-up store and more. Digital gifting outperforms traditional models at $40 gifting budgets and above. It's the right fit if you're constrained on space or labor, serve a wide database where one gift never fits everyone, or want to run gifting without a warehouse operation behind it.

WHY IT WORKS

The ROI Lever

Digital gifting at $40+ does two jobs at once. It gives the property a strategic loyalty lever for higher-value players who aren't quite VIPs yet — guests with strong play and real play-up potential who see no value in a typical $15 continuity gift. Give them something they'll actually use, something they haven't already been gifted, and their play behavior changes.

That's the gift doing more than driving redemption. It builds guest loyalty and brand sentiment — the kind your competitor won't win back with their promotion plan anytime soon.

Insight to Know
Start reinvestment low, build it over time.

Digital's biggest strength — variety — is also its catch. Guests see items side by side each month and develop a sharp sense of value fast, even with no price tags anywhere. Start reinvestment conservatively and build it up; pulling back once guests are used to a level costs more in redemptions and sentiment than starting low ever would.
